Explains how companies can use a marginal abatement cost (MACC) curve to prioritize decarbonization investments by ranking projects by cost per tonne of CO2e removed. The framework helps organizations shift from net-zero ambitions to identifying genuinely profitable decarbonization opportunities with positive financial returns that can fund longer-term climate strategies.

SINAI's Utility Automation addresses a common enterprise bottleneck by automating the flow of electricity, gas, water, and waste consumption data directly from utility providers into emissions inventories, even when bills arrive on different cycles, formats, and languages. SINAI's CEO notes that "utility data is where decarbonization quietly stalls" with teams losing weeks each quarter turning bills into defensible numbers, and the solution shifts focus from data collection to actionable insights.
